Delete your WeekendOut account & data

Last updated: 9 May 2026 · Developer: Akhil Raj S · App: WeekendOut

This page explains how to permanently delete your WeekendOut account and the data WeekendOut has stored against it.

Steps to request deletion

  1. Email akhilrajsanthosh@gmail.com with the subject line WeekendOut account deletion.
  2. In the body, include the email address you used to sign in to WeekendOut with Google (so we can locate your record).
  3. We will reply within 7 days confirming that your account and all associated data have been deleted from our systems.

If you have only used the app anonymously (you never tapped "Continue with Google" on the save prompt), there is nothing tied to a personal identifier on our side. Uninstalling the app from your phone will remove the only state about you that exists.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens on deletion
Your Google sign-in linkage (email + display name) Permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication
Saved places list Permanently deleted from Firestore
Last-used planner preferences Permanently deleted from Firestore
Recommendation history (if any) Permanently deleted from Firestore

What is kept

DataWhyHow long
Anonymized request logs (no email, no UID) Operational debugging and abuse prevention 30 days, then auto-purged
Aggregated, non-identifying counts (e.g. "X recommendations served today") Capacity planning Indefinite, but not tied to any user

Nothing personally identifying is retained after deletion. We do not sell or share user data with third parties; see our Privacy Policy for full details.

Questions
